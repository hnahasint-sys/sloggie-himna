# 10-Day Organic Growth Sprint

No ad spend. No collabs. Just you, a phone camera, and 10 days.

**The rule:** film solo, post daily, let automation handle the boring parts (scheduling and DM replies) so you can spend your time on content, not busywork.

---

## Before Day 1

- [ ] Pick 3 platforms max (start with TikTok + Instagram Reels, add a 3rd only if you have gas left)
- [ ] Set up a posting schedule tool so you can batch-film and auto-post (check IFTTT or Metricool if you want this connected here in Claude)
- [ ] Write down ONE slogan or tee you're pushing hardest this sprint

Think of this like a relay race. Day 1 hands off to Day 2. Don't skip the baton pass.

---

## Days 1-3: Hook Phase

**Goal:** Get eyes on the brand. Not sales yet, just attention.

- **Day 1:** Origin story video. Why "Sloggie Himna" exists, why the slogans matter to you.
- **Day 2:** Show the product close up. Fabric, print quality, how it fits.
- **Day 3:** Behind-the-scenes. Film yourself packing an order or designing a slogan.

**Daily task:** 1 post per platform, reply to every comment within a few hours.

---

## Days 4-6: Proof Phase

**Goal:** Build trust. Show real reactions, real use.

- **Day 4:** Customer reaction video (or a friend trying it on, if no customer footage yet)
- **Day 5:** "Why I picked this slogan" explainer, Gen Z pain point tie-in
- **Day 6:** Comparison video: your tee vs. a generic plain tee. Make the difference obvious.

**Daily task:** Reply to every comment and DM. Use your abandoned cart email sequence as a safety net for anyone who clicks but doesn't buy.

---

## Days 7-9: Push Phase

**Goal:** Turn attention into orders.

- **Day 7:** Announce a limited discount code (pull from your 12 active codes)
- **Day 8:** Countdown post. Urgency without being pushy. "Code ends in 48 hours."
- **Day 9:** Last call post. Show your top comment or DM reaction from the week.

**Daily task:** Check your Google Sheets dashboard each morning. If a discount code isn't converting, swap it out.

---

## Day 10: Review Phase

- [ ] Pull the week's numbers from your dashboard: views, clicks, sales, new subscribers
- [ ] Mark which video got the most engagement. That's your format for next sprint.
- [ ] Log what discount code performed best
- [ ] Set up your next 10-day sprint using the winning format

---

## The 3-point check for every post

1. **Is it simple?** Could a stranger understand it in 3 seconds?
2. **Does it show the product?** No product, no sale.
3. **Is there a next step?** Link in bio, code in caption, something to click.

If a video fails one of these, don't post it. Reshoot instead.

## Reminder

You're doing this solo. That's a strength, not a weakness. Raw beats polished on these platforms right now. Don't wait for the "perfect" shot. Post the real one.
